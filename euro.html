Sources & data notes

Your itinerary is transcribed from supplied reservation PDFs. A booking status means evidence in those documents, not a live reconfirmation. Original PDFs and their booking codes are deliberately excluded from the app.

Application JavaScript, trip data and the cover photo are embedded in this HTML. Online weather, currency, news, map tiles and web fonts need internet. If online data fails, an explicitly dated cached value may be shown. Opening the saved HTML allows itinerary access offline. The installed app can reopen a previously saved itinerary offline after a successful online visit; online feeds still require internet.

Only public city coordinates and city news keywords are sent to data services. No names, hotel reservations, notes or checklists are included in those requests. Notes, saved news and checkmarks use browser storage; they do not sync across devices. The private hosted site requires authorized sign-in. The installed app may keep an itinerary copy on this device for offline use; a downloaded HTML copy has no access gate. Other companions need their own authorized access or a copy you choose to share.

Trip documents used: the two Iberia e-tickets and seat receipt; Vienna restaurant, KHM café and Karlskirche confirmations; seven hotel confirmations; ÖBB Vienna–Hallstatt ticket; Hallstatt–Bad Ischl and Bad Ischl–Munich timetables; DB Munich–Nuremberg ticket; FlixBus Nuremberg–Regensburg voucher; Regensburg–Prague tickets; Prague–Vienna ticket PDF. Flight gates, platforms, opening times and booking validity require current operator checks.
